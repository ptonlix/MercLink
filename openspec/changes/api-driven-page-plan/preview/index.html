商品介绍

Discover Chengdu beyond the traditional old-city night walks. This romantic evening adventure combines local street food, sunset cycling and Chengdu’s most photogenic modern landmarks. Ride beside the Jinjiang River, find hidden skyline viewpoints and experience the spectacular Twin Towers and viral Bamboo Fountain—all glowing after dark.

行程体验

  • Sanse Road Night Market. Explore colourful food stalls beside the Jinjiang River and taste the street snacks Chengdu locals love.
  • Sunset riverside cycling. Ride as the city lights come on. At Jiuye Lotus Bridge, capture the water, bridge and skyline together.
  • Jiaozi Park & Twin Towers. Discover Chengdu’s connection to the world’s earliest paper money and enjoy hidden viewpoints for the illuminated towers.
  • Bamboo Fountain. Finish with water, light and technology at one of Chengdu’s most memorable nighttime landmarks.
